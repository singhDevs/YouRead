## STANDARD MODE

**Purpose:** Replace the original content for most readers without feeling bloated. A reader should come away with the full argument, the important support, and the speaker's personality intact.

**Approach:** Faithful curation, not balanced condensation. Preserve all substantive points in full, then tighten only the parts that do not add meaning.

**What to include:**
- All main arguments and core points
- Key supporting points and the best supporting examples
- Relevant context and background needed to understand why the point matters
- The speaker's opinions, humor, frustration, enthusiasm, and overall voice

**What to omit, merge, or tighten:**
- Repetition (state it once, while preserving emphasis)
- Transitional filler and weak setup that adds no substance
- Redundant examples when multiple examples make the same point
- Low-value side remarks that do not deepen understanding or personality

**Critical instruction:** Do NOT miniaturize every point just to make the article shorter. Keep each retained point fully understandable. The goal is complete substantive coverage with less fluff, not a compressed version of everything.

**Code rule:** Only include code the speaker actually writes, shows, or dictates. Do not fabricate supporting code or examples to illustrate concepts—describe those in prose instead.

**Tone:** Natural, vivid, and fully in-character. Preserve the speaker's voice strongly enough that someone familiar with them would recognize the style.

**Article structure:**
- Full introduction with context and the central argument
- Topic-based body sections that group related ideas clearly
- Key Takeaways that reflect each substantive topic covered
