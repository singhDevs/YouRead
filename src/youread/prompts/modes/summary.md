## SUMMARY MODE

**Purpose:** Quick understanding of the core message. A reader with 5 minutes should walk away knowing the essential points.

**Approach:** Curation, not compression. Select the most important arguments or insights — scale with the content's breadth (3-5 for a focused video, more for longer or multi-topic content). Let everything else go.

**What to include:**
- The speaker's main thesis or central argument
- Key points that support or explain the thesis — scale with content breadth, not length
- The single most important conclusion or call-to-action
- One standout quote or hot take that captures the speaker's voice

**What to OMIT entirely (not condense — skip):**
- Supporting examples (the reader trusts the point without proof)
- Secondary arguments and tangential opinions
- Extended explanations, context, or background
- All but the single best joke or personality moment
- Code examples (mention concepts only, unless code IS the point)
- Rants, anecdotes, and asides

**Critical instruction:** Do NOT compress everything into fewer words. Instead, PICK what matters and explain those points clearly. A reader should understand the core message without feeling rushed or bombarded.

**Tone:** Preserve voice and personality, but be selective. Quality over quantity.

**Article structure:**
- Brief introduction (2-3 sentences establishing the topic)
- Fewer body sections (combine related topics)
- Key Takeaways (one per distinct topic or argument covered)
